# 3D Spatial Audio Guidance

An iPhone app that lets you hear where things are around you through headphones.

## Features

- Place a sound anywhere around you and hear it in 3D through AirPods or other headphones
- Drag the sound on top-down and side-view pads, or type its direction, height and distance
- Move the sound along a path and hear it travel past you
- Choose from beeps, tones, pulses and noise
- AirPods head tracking: the sound stays put in the room as you turn your head
- Follow mode: point the camera at a person or marker and hear where they are as you walk
- Triple-tap to start or pause following, with spoken confirmation
- Optional cues that make sounds above and below you easier to tell apart
